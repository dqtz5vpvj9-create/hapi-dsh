The fix separates newly admitted content from retained history. Complete-window readers charge new/revised source entries and stable derived Context identities against the unchanged admission limits. The HHPI provider retains accepted contiguous pages instead of trimming their opposite edge to satisfy an opening envelope. Default bounded-window hosts retain their original absolute limits. See [the admission ADR](adr/hhpi-continuous-admission.md) and [upstream proposal](upstream-pr-continuous-admission.md).
